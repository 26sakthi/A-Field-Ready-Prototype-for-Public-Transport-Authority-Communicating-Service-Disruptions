VERITRANSIT PROJECT - PHASE 1 REPORT: FOUNDATIONS, ARCHITECTURE, AND CORE ENGINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. INTRODUCTION & PROJECT OVERVIEW
VeriTransit is a field-ready prototype designed to assist public transport authorities in communicating service disruptions. The primary challenge addressed is the inability of decision-makers to verify crowd-sourced reports quickly during critical incidents. VeriTransit ingests simulated citizen reports, clusters them, scores them with an explainable confidence metric, ranks them by priority, and surfaces verified, high-priority incidents rapidly. 

2. PHASE 1 GOALS & FOUNDATIONS
The objective of Phase 1 was to establish the system's foundational architecture, define the baseline metrics for later comparison, and develop the core algorithmic engine. 

To ensure the prototype is a robust, end-to-end system rather than an isolated model, the technology stack was strictly defined:
- Backend: Python 3.11, FastAPI, WebSockets, and SQLite for lightweight, reliable data storage.
- Frontend: React 18 with Vite and TypeScript for building the five role-based views.
- Core Engine: A deterministic, rule-based pure-Python package designed for rigorous testing and explainability.

During the foundational stage (Phase 0), the repository was scaffolded, and a baseline triage policy (FIFO/chronological without clustering or confidence scoring) was established. This baseline mimics the existing manual process of reading reports as they arrive, providing a benchmark that the VeriTransit system must out-perform in later experimental phases.

3. CORE ENGINE SLICE
The algorithmic heart of VeriTransit was developed in this phase. The engine operates on deterministic rules to ensure every confidence score can be decomposed into named signals ("why this score"), making the system highly transparent for human operators.

Key modules implemented include:
- Clustering (cluster.py): Groups raw citizen reports based on spatial (location), temporal (time), and categorical proximity. This reduces noise and transforms hundreds of disparate reports into distinct incidents.
- Independence & Deduping (independence.py): Filters out coordinated misinformation bursts, deduplicates identical reports, and weighs reporter reputation.
- Confidence Scoring (confidence.py): Calculates an overall confidence score using weighted signals such as corroborating sources, responder verification, and official feed matches.
- Priority & States (priority.py, states.py): Ranks incidents and transitions them through a state machine (e.g., from unverified to verified, disputed, or stale).

4. PHASE 1 OUTCOMES
By the conclusion of Phase 1, the core algorithm and rules were fully unit-tested and integrated with the ingestion API. Injecting a scripted stream of raw reports successfully produces correctly clustered, scored, and prioritized incidents, proving the viability of the backend processing engine.
