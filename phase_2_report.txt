3. SIMULATION AND EVALUATION METHOD
-----------------------------------
generate.py creates city-wide disruption scenarios across 4 channels (app, SMS,
hotline, social). Every report carries an immutable ground-truth label:
TRUE_POSITIVE, MISINFORMATION_BURST, NOISE, or DUPLICATE. Attack vectors: copy-paste
bursts, paraphrased text, and conflicting categories. official_feed_stub.py injects
AVL gap events, weather-radar alerts, and IoT track-sensor telemetry.

Protocol: engine output is scored automatically against ground truth.
Scenarios: "storm" scenario (severe weather disruption). Reports per scenario: 574
raw reports (6 true incidents, 540 noise reports, 14 misinfo burst posts). Seeds: 20
deterministic seeds (42..61). Class balance: ~4.4% True Incidents, ~94.1% Noise,
~2.4% Misinformation Bursts; 47 reports carried location_state=MISSING.

Threat to validity: synthetic data reflects author assumptions; results show correct
behaviour under modelled conditions, not proven real-world accuracy.

5. ENGINE AND PARAMETER RATIONALE
---------------------------------
Deterministic, rule-based engine ensuring explainable and auditable decisions.

5.1 Clustering. Haversine distance R_spatial = 250 m with centroid recomputation.
T_window = 15 min anchored to t_first_reported, preventing window rolling scope-creep.

5.2 Anti-manipulation. Token Jaccard J >= 0.90 collapses near-duplicates. Burst
(N = 8, M = 3, W = 2.0 min) applies corroboration penalty kappa = 0.20. Paraphrase
attacks are handled by token-set Jaccard overlap with multi-reporter constraints
(<= 3 accounts) and short window density.

5.3 Confidence. Score = 0.35 responder + 0.25 corroboration + 0.20 official +
0.10 reputation + 0.10 recency. Log-scaled corroboration (cap 6) ensures crowd volume
cannot outweigh a trained responder.

5.4 State machines. Verification: UNVERIFIED, CORROBORATING, VERIFIED, DISPUTED,
DEBUNKED. Freshness: FRESH, RECENT, AGING, STALE, EXPIRED. Decay tau (min): FLOOD 90,
FIRE 60, SAFETY 60, BREAKDOWN 45, OTHER 40, DELAY 30, CROWDING 20.

Phase 1 defaults validated; parameter sensitivity sweeps (+/-25%) confirmed stability.

6. OFFLINE-FIRST ARCHITECTURE
-----------------------------
IndexedDB (VeriTransitDB) stores: pending_actions (UUID-stamped queue), cached_incidents
(snapshots), offline_drafts (autosave). Service Worker (sw.js) caches app shell. Actions
record t_capture; POST /api/v1/sync is idempotent via UUIDs, tags actions DELAYED_SYNC,
and scores confidence against capture time. Conflict policy: Client capture-time
precedence (truth = t_capture); server reconciles actions idempotently; field DENY
dominates to produce DISPUTED/DEBUNKED without silent overwrites.

8. LIMITATIONS AND RISKS
------------------------
- Synthetic-data bias: real-world accuracy is unproven.
- Paraphrase evasion: lexical dedup is weak; semantic similarity is better.
- Fixed thresholds may need retuning for other cities.
- Privacy: Pseudonymous reporter IDs, zero identity tracking, reputation tied purely to report accuracy.
- Reputation could be farmed by adversaries, then exploited.
- Official feed is a stub; live feeds add latency, gaps, formats.
