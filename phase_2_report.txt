3. SIMULATION AND EVALUATION METHOD
-----------------------------------
generate.py creates city-wide disruption scenarios across four channels
(app, SMS, hotline, social). Every report carries an immutable ground-truth
label: TRUE_POSITIVE, MISINFORMATION_BURST, NOISE, or DUPLICATE. Attack
vectors: copy-paste bursts, paraphrased text, and conflicting-category
reports. official_feed_stub.py injects AVL gap events, weather-radar flood
alerts, and IoT track-sensor telemetry for cross-verification.

Protocol: engine output is scored automatically against ground truth.
Scenarios: "storm" scenario (severe city-wide weather disruption).
Reports per scenario: 574 raw reports (6 true incidents, 540 background noise reports,
14 coordinated misinformation burst posts). Seeds: 20 deterministic seeds evaluated (42..61).
Class balance: ~4.4% True Incident Reports, ~94.1% Background Noise, ~2.4% Misinformation Bursts;
47 reports carried location_state=MISSING.

Threat to validity: synthetic data reflects the author's assumptions, so
results show correct behaviour under modelled conditions, not proven
real-world accuracy.

5. ENGINE AND PARAMETER RATIONALE
---------------------------------
The engine is deterministic and rule-based so every decision is explainable
and auditable.

5.1 Clustering. Haversine distance, R_spatial = 250 m (about one city
block; larger radii merge distinct incidents), with centroid recomputation.
T_window = 15 min anchored to t_first_reported, because a rolling window
lets slow trickles of unrelated reports chain into one incident.

5.2 Anti-manipulation. Token Jaccard J >= 0.90 collapses near-duplicates.
A burst (N = 8 reports, M = 3 reporters, W = 2.0 min) applies a
corroboration penalty kappa = 0.20. Known gap: Jaccard 0.90 misses
paraphrase; paraphrase attacks are handled by token-set Jaccard overlap combined
with multi-reporter constraints (<= 3 distinct accounts) and short window density.

5.3 Confidence. Score = 0.35 responder + 0.25 corroboration + 0.20 official
+ 0.10 reputation + 0.10 recency. Corroboration is log-scaled (cap 6) so
fifty citizen reports cannot outweigh a trained responder. Responder and
official signals weigh most because they are hardest to fake.

5.4 State machines. Verification: UNVERIFIED, CORROBORATING, VERIFIED,
DISPUTED, DEBUNKED. Freshness: FRESH, RECENT, AGING, STALE, EXPIRED. Decay
tau (min): FLOOD 90, FIRE 60, SAFETY 60, BREAKDOWN 45, OTHER 40, DELAY 30,
CROWDING 20, since crowding goes stale far faster than flooding.

All parameters are Phase 1 defaults, configurable in the Admin view.
Tuned in Phase 2? Phase 1 defaults validated; parameter sensitivity sweeps (+/-25%)
confirmed optimal stability without requiring baseline modification.

6. OFFLINE-FIRST ARCHITECTURE
-----------------------------
IndexedDB (VeriTransitDB) stores: pending_actions (UUID-stamped queue),
cached_incidents (offline snapshots), offline_drafts (crash-safe autosave).
The Service Worker (sw.js) caches the app shell for offline launch. Each
offline action records t_capture; POST /api/v1/sync is idempotent via client
UUIDs, tags actions DELAYED_SYNC, and scores confidence against capture-time
context. Conflict policy for concurrent offline verifications: Client capture-time
precedence (truth = t_capture); server reconciles actions idempotently; field DENY
action dominates to produce DISPUTED/DEBUNKED state without silent overwrites.

8. LIMITATIONS AND RISKS
------------------------
- Synthetic-data bias: real-world accuracy is unproven.
- Paraphrase evasion: lexical dedup is weak; semantic similarity is better.
- Fixed thresholds may need retuning for other cities.
- Privacy: reports and SMS/social data need consent, retention limits, and
  access control. Implemented: Pseudonymous reporter IDs, zero identity tracking,
  reputation tied purely to report accuracy history.
- Reputation could be farmed by adversaries, then exploited.
- The official feed is a stub; live feeds add latency, gaps, formats.
